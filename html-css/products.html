Discover our water variety

Tap water

Why climb a mountain for a spring when the spring comes to you? Our tap water is cold, reliable, and ready at 3 a.m. when you wake up thirsty and confused. No cape, no battery, no subscription, just pure, wet greatness. Vesitori salutes you.

Rain water

Why settle for water that has been sitting in pipes when you can have water that just fell from the clouds? Our rain water is collected fresh, fills your glass with a little sparkle of sky, and arrives with its own bragging rights. Tap water is a loyal friend, but rain water is its famous cousin who travels and has stories to tell. Vesitori: we catch the sky so you don't have to.

Canned water

Some water likes to travel light. Ours puts on a tiny metal suit, hops into a can, and waits patiently for you to go psssht. It fits in your bag, survives the bus, and never spills in your backpack (we are mostly sure). Rain water fell from the sky, tap water came through the pipes, and canned water is the cool one that shows up with its own packaging and a sense of style. Vesitori: because even water deserves a little armor.
